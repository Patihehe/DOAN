=== SOC Lab 2: Phong thu SYN Flood & DDoS Rate Limiting (Wazuh SIEM) ===

Ke tan cong tu dong (172.20.0.5) dang ban bao toi TCP SYN Flood (hping3) sang may "target" (172.20.0.10)
tren port 80, lam cạn kiet socket va nghen dich vu Web cua cong ty.

Nhiem vu cua ban: DIEU TRA va UNG PHO tu tram DEFENDER de bao ve dich vu Web.

--- 1) Giam sat tren SIEM (Dashboard Firefox tren Defender) ---
  https://172.20.0.20   (admin / pass trong /root/wazuh-admin-credentials.txt tren siem)
  -> Threat Hunting: Quan sat so luong goi tin TCP SYN va canh bao luong traffic gia tang tu IP 172.20.0.5.

--- 2) Dieu tra & Ung pho (SSH tu defender sang target) ---
  ssh analyst@target            (mat khau: analyst)

  Phuong an A - Cau hinh Rate Limiting voi iptables (Khuyen nghi):
    Cho phep luong goi SYN voi toc do gioi han (vi du limit BURST_VAL/s) va drop traffic vuot nguong:
    sudo iptables -A INPUT -p tcp --syn --dport 80 -m limit --limit 5/s --limit-burst BURST_VAL -j ACCEPT
    sudo iptables -A INPUT -p tcp --syn --dport 80 -j DROP

  Phuong an B - Bat TCP Syncookies:
    sudo sysctl -w net.ipv4.tcp_syncookies=1

--- 3) Kiem chứng ---
  Sau khi ap dung giai phap, kiem tra dich vu Web van phan hoi binh thuong:
    curl -I http://localhost:80
